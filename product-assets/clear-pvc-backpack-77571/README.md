# Product 77571 — custom clear PVC backpack

Prepared product listing and import assets for the transparent backpack product. The prepared localized carousel galleries are in `apps/storefront/public/vibeya/assets/products/clear-pvc-backpack-77571/`.

## Product details
- Clear PVC body, black trim, zipper, straps, top handle and mesh side pockets.
- Color: Clear. Size: One Size.
- Draft dimensions: 43 × 32 × 13 cm (16.9 × 12.6 × 5.1 in).
- Front panel can be customized; back remains clear and unprinted.
- Custom artwork can be uploaded or described in a prompt and previewed before ordering.
- Ships from China with free shipping to the US, Canada, Mexico, and Japan.
