Bubble Bath Cats brings the familiar charm of curious cats into a bubbly bathtub scene. Ginger, charcoal, gray, and cream cats float, stretch, and snooze among soft blue bubbles on a warm beige ground. The simple hand-drawn outlines keep this cute cat shower curtain cheerful and easy to enjoy in an everyday bathroom.

A playful cat shower curtain in warm neutral colors

The beige background gives the busy cat illustration a softer, more relaxed feel, while the pale blue bubbles add a fresh pop of color. A tub full of cats anchors the lower part of the artwork, with smaller cats and bubbles spread above it to create a balanced, full-height print.

Example design prompt

Create a full-height custom shower curtain print on a warm beige background. Fill the lower third with a simple white bathtub overflowing with soft pale-blue bubbles and relaxed cream, ginger, gray, and charcoal cats. Scatter smaller playful cats and translucent soap bubbles above the tub, with clean hand-drawn black outlines, gentle muted colors, and a cozy cute bathroom decor mood. Balance the art across the full portrait canvas, keep the main cat faces clear, and avoid a border, lettering, logos, or mockup.
