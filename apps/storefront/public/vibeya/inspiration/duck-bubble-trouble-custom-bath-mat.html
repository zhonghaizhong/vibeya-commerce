Duck Bubble Trouble turns a familiar rubber duck into an oversized, silly centerpiece. Glossy turquoise bubbles, foam stars, and a few coral splashes create a sense of motion around the duck, giving this funny bath mat design an eye-catching 3D optical illusion feel.

A playful rubber duck bath mat with a bold focal point

The duck’s warm yellow color contrasts with the cool blue-green bubbles, so the main character reads quickly from across the room. Rounded shapes and bright highlights keep the artwork cheerful, while small coral accents add energy without competing with the duck.
