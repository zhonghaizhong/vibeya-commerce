Example design prompt

Create a clean funny penguin face graphic for the front of a black women's tankini swimsuit: one large round eye, one half-lidded eye, cream face patches, and a bright orange beak. Use crisp dark outlines, a compact centered composition, and no words, logos, background, or extra objects. Keep the matching side-tie swim shorts plain black.
