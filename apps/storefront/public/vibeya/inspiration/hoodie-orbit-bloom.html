Orbit Bloom turns a soft washed hoodie into an expressive floral graphic hoodie, pairing a bold flower with orbiting dots and a quiet crescent. Explore this original custom hoodie design, compare six garment colors, and choose front, back, or double-sided print placement.

Orbit Bloom pairs a familiar botanical shape with oversized circular accents and a small moon. It takes cues from current interest in expressive dots and nature-led, escapist styling, then turns them into a new, original chest graphic for the washed lavender VIBEYA hoodie.

1. Make the Shape Read from Across the Room

The large flower gives the design an easy focal point. Round orbit dots add movement and a playful rhythm, while smaller ink lines keep the petals from feeling too flat. The layout stays centered and compact enough to sit clearly above the kangaroo pocket.

2. Build Contrast for Lavender Fleece

Warm ivory and peach lift the flower from the purple fabric. Deep plum defines its outline, and chartreuse leaves give the graphic a sharp accent that echoes VIBEYA’s energetic visual language. The palette is limited so the print remains legible on the garment.

Create the same original botanical-celestial chest print for a VIBEYA washed pullover hoodie in the currently selected garment color.
