This funny custom blanket turns a soft flannel throw into a cozy bedtime scene. A kind Jesus keeps gentle watch while a dreamer and sleepy sheep rest among clouds, stars, and a smiling crescent moon.

A cozy blanket gift with a little humor

“Sleep Well, Dream Big” and “No nightmares tonight” give this playful sleep gift a reassuring, lighthearted tone. It works as a custom throw blanket idea for relaxing on the sofa or winding down at bedtime.

Designed for a full-surface blanket print

The VIBEYA flannel throw prints across the entire front surface, with a soft white reverse. This artwork is composed to fill the blanket face edge to edge, rather than sit as a small centered patch. Available product sizes are 30 × 40, 40 × 50, 50 × 60, and 60 × 80 inches.

Example design prompt

Create a funny, comforting full-surface print for a custom flannel throw blanket: a kind Jesus as a gentle night guardian watching over a peacefully sleeping person among soft clouds, a smiling crescent moon, stars, and sleepy sheep in nightcaps. Add the exact phrases “SLEEP WELL, DREAM BIG” and “No nightmares tonight.” Use cozy storybook illustration, warm cream, midnight navy, lavender, sky blue, and muted gold. Fill the entire blanket print area edge to edge with no border, no mockup.
